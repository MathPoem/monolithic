Generous Auction · playground, geometric one-shot draw

model of generous-auction.md

A single draw distributes R tokens across the standing book: each tick's bar is a stack of its bids, and the draw splits tokens by weights w = q^(τ−i). A tick whose budget runs out mid-draw is capped — its share re-flows upward through the waterfall. Everything recomputes live as you edit bids or parameters. Click a tick to see its calculation with real numbers substituted.

Parameters

decay q = (weight of the next tick down) R = tokens in the draw floor = USDG tick spacing = bps — price(i) = floor·(1+bps/10⁴)^i

Book

tick = budget = USDG

Waterfall of the draw

Book & draw

left axis (colour) — tick budgets; bright part is what the draw spends, dimmed is left unspent · right axis (grey): with the curve on — dashed track = max buyable (budget/price), solid part = received, translucent top = overflow spilling onward; with the curve off — the whole bar is the tick's token demand · dashed curve — the initial pour R·q^(τ−x)/W · solid curve — the effective envelope R·G·q^(τ−x): it passes exactly through every un-capped bar · floor on the left, price increases to the right

What we compute — the draw formula

the on-chain lazy computation folds the whole waterfall into one accumulator: dG = Δ/W per stage, consumed by tick i = q^(τ−i)·R·ΔG (generous-auction.md §3)
